My Story

I come from a Mexican family and have grown up with a unique connection to both Mexican and American cultures. I lived in Mexico for a decade while attending school in the United States, and I now live and study in the U.S. This experience has shaped the way I see people, cultures, and the world around me. My family, especially my mother, has had the greatest influence on who I am. She is my role model and taught me the importance of courage and facing life with determination. The people I have met throughout my life have also played an important role in shaping my perspective and helping me understand different ways of thinking. I have always been curious about technology and how things work, particularly when that knowledge can be used to accomplish something meaningful. Since I was young, I've been fascinated by the possibility of creating technology that can understand and replicate aspects of human behavior and decision-making. Today, I continue exploring technology while also developing my interests in investing, music, animals, and the world around me.
